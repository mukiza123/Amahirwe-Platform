About Amahirwe

Helping students discover what they're good at.

Amahirwe is a platform designed for students in rural public schools in Rwanda to discover their talents and connect with mentors, teachers and opportunities — built around the idea that every student has something worth discovering.

What is Amahirwe?

Amahirwe is a digital platform that helps students identify their strengths through a simple, offline-friendly talent assessment. Once a student's talent area is clearer, the platform can suggest a verified mentor and surface opportunities — scholarships, competitions and internships — related to that area.

It isn't a replacement for school, and it isn't a general e-learning app. Its purpose is narrower and more specific: turning a student's hidden talent into something recognised, supported, and connected to a real next step.

Why we started

Students in rural schools often have real talents in areas like technology, art, sport or public speaking — but fewer chances to have those talents noticed, guided or connected to an opportunity. Most existing tools are either general e-learning platforms or career services centred on cities.

Amahirwe is designed to work alongside schools rather than around them, starting with a focused pilot in Rwanda's Eastern Province before considering a wider rollout.

Who we serve

Amahirwe is built around five groups working together.

Students

Ages 12–19 in rural public schools, discovering their strengths.

Schools

The trusted local base that grounds the whole platform.

Teachers

The trusted adults who confirm profiles and review matches.

Mentors

Verified volunteers offering guidance in their field.

Opportunity providers

Schools, NGOs and organisations posting real opportunities.

Our approach

Everything on Amahirwe follows the same three-step idea.

1

Discover

A student explores their interests through a short, friendly assessment.

2

Connect

A teacher-approved match links the student with a verified mentor.

3

Grow

The student tracks progress and discovers opportunities suited to them.
