How It Works

From a talent assessment to a real opportunity.

A step-by-step look at what happens on Amahirwe, designed to be easy to follow, even if this is the first time you've heard about the platform.

Six steps, one student at a time.

Every part of Amahirwe exists to move a student through this sequence safely, at their own pace, and without needing a constant internet connection to get started.

Discover

The student completes a short talent assessment (mixing text, images and optional voice notes) which works fully offline.

Build

A basic profile is created around the student's interests and strengths, started by the student or, if they're shy about it, by a supervising teacher.

Match

Amahirwe identifies potentially relevant mentors from a verified pool, using a simple, transparent match on talent area and language.

Review

A teacher or administrator reviews the suggested mentor connection before anything moves forward.

Connect

Once approved, the mentorship connection is created, and both the mentor and the student's teacher are notified.

Grow

The student discovers opportunities suited to their talent area and tracks their progress and milestones over time.

Safety comes first

A mentor never receives contact details without teacher or administrator approval first. Because Amahirwe's primary users are minors, this step is never skipped.
