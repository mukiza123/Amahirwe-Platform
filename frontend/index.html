Every student has potential.

Many students have talents and interests that aren't always recognised or connected to opportunity, especially in schools with fewer resources. Amahirwe is designed to help make those connections possible.

How Amahirwe works

Three simple steps, built to work even without the internet.

01

Discover

Students explore their interests and strengths through a simple talent assessment.

02

Connect

Students can be connected with relevant mentors through a supervised matching process.

03

Grow

Students discover opportunities and continue building their skills and achievements.

Our projected impact

Amahirwe is a pilot project. Here's what it's designed to achieve.

More talent discovered

Helping students recognise strengths that may otherwise remain hidden.

Stronger mentorship

Creating structured connections between students and trusted mentors.

Greater access to opportunity

Making scholarships, competitions, internships and other opportunities easier to discover.

Stronger school support

Giving teachers better visibility into student progress and areas where support may be needed.

Help us create more pathways for young talent.

Amahirwe is designed to bring students, schools, mentors and opportunity providers closer together.
