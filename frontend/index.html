Why Amahirwe matters

Many talented students in rural schools never get the chance to be noticed. Amahirwe gives every student a simple way to be seen, supported, and connected to people who can help them grow — in Kinyarwanda first.

Opportunities and mentorship

Verified mentors and opportunity providers post scholarships, competitions and internships. Every mentor match is reviewed by a teacher first, to keep students safe.

Mentorship

Verified mentors
