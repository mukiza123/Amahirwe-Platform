For Mentors

Your experience could help shape someone's next step.

Mentoring on Amahirwe is a light, structured commitment, not an open-ended one. Here's what it realistically involves.

Share what you know

Mentors bring experience that students may not have access to in their immediate environment.

Help students explore

Help young people understand different careers and possibilities open to them.

Build confidence

Encourage students to believe their interests can become real skills.

Give back

Support the next generation of students working toward their own path.

How mentoring works

After registering, mentors go through a verification step by an administrator. Once verified, a mentor may be suggested to a student based on a simple match of talent area and language. That match still needs a teacher or administrator's approval before it becomes active. Mentors don't search for students, and students don't search for mentors on their own.

Mentorship with safeguards

Because Amahirwe's students are minors, mentorship is built around safeguards from the start:

  • Mentors are expected to be verified by an administrator before appearing in any match.
  • Every mentor connection requires teacher or administrator approval before it becomes active.
  • Students are not given unrestricted private communication with mentors.
