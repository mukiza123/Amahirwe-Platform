Our Mission

Turning potential into a real next step.

Supporting young people to discover their strengths and connect those strengths to people, resources and opportunities.

Our vision

A future where talent is not limited by where a young person grows up, where a student's location or circumstances don't determine whether their strengths are noticed and supported.

What we believe

Every student has potential.

Talent isn't rare; the chance to have it noticed and supported often is.

Opportunity should be easier to discover.

Scholarships, competitions and internships shouldn't depend on who you already know.

Schools can play an important role.

Teachers are often best placed to notice a student's strengths and vouch for them.

Mentorship helps young people choose well.

A conversation with someone further along a path can change how a student sees their own options.

Technology can help bridge opportunity gaps.

Used carefully, and designed for low connectivity, it can reach students other tools don't.

Our goals

What this first phase of Amahirwe is working toward.

1

Increase talent discovery among students

Give more students a simple way to identify their strengths, even without a stable internet connection.

2

Create structured connections with trusted mentors

Match students to verified mentors, with a teacher or administrator approving every connection.

3

Improve access to opportunities

Surface scholarships, competitions, internships and training relevant to each student's talent area.

4

Help teachers identify students who need encouragement

Give teachers visibility into which students have engaged, and which may need a nudge.

5

Build a model that can expand over time

Start with a focused Rwanda pilot, with an architecture designed to grow beyond it.
