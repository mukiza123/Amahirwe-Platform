Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Prototype version

Amahirwe is a university software-engineering prototype. This page explains what account data the platform collects and how it's used within the demo. Because this is a prototype rather than a commercial product, some practices described (such as data retention and deletion) are simplified compared to what a production deployment would need.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details — full name, email address, password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), and role (student, teacher, mentor, opportunity provider, parent/guardian or admin).
  • Profile information — school, grade, subject area, or organisation, depending on your role.
  • Activity data — talent assessment answers and results, mentor-match requests, and messages you send within the platform.
  • Technical data — basic request information (such as timestamps) needed to keep the service secure and working.

2. Email verification

When you register, Amahirwe generates a one-time 6-digit verification code tied to your account. In this prototype, the code is shown directly on the verification page instead of being sent through a real email provider, since no mail service is connected yet. In a production deployment, this code would instead be delivered only to the email address you registered with.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, including verifying that you control the email address provided.
  • To match students with mentors and relevant opportunities.
  • To let teachers and administrators review and approve connections, consistent with Amahirwe's school-anchored design.
  • To show you your own dashboard, progress and history within the platform.

4. Who can see your information

Your profile is visible to the teachers, mentors, or administrators involved in your matches, as appropriate for your role. Amahirwe does not sell personal data, and does not share it with third parties outside of the platform's own mentorship and opportunity features.

5. Data retention and deletion

As a prototype, demo data (including test accounts) may be periodically reset. You can request account deletion at any time through the contact page.

6. Children's privacy

Amahirwe is designed for use by students aged roughly 12–19 within a school-anchored program, with teacher and guardian visibility built into the platform's design rather than treated as an afterthought.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the prototype evolves. Continued use of the platform after a change means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
