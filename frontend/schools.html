For Schools

Welcome, schools.

Supporting students starts with recognising their potential. Amahirwe works with schools as an important bridge between students, mentors and opportunities.

What schools can do

  • Help students complete their profiles
  • Monitor student progress
  • Recommend students who may be shy about registering themselves
  • Review mentor matches before any contact is made
  • Help students discover opportunities suited to them

For teachers

The teacher dashboard is built around one question: which students need attention right now? It shows, at a glance, who has completed a profile, who has a mentor match pending approval, and who may need a nudge to finish their talent assessment, without burying that under menus.

For school leaders

Beyond individual classrooms, Amahirwe is designed to give school leadership better visibility into where students' interests lie, how engagement is trending, and which external opportunities students are being connected to, without turning into a complex reporting tool.
