Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026 · Prototype version

Amahirwe is a university software-engineering prototype built to demonstrate a student-mentorship platform for Rwanda. These terms describe how the demo may be used. Because this is a prototype and not a live commercial service, some sections describe how a production version would work rather than guarantees this build can currently keep.

1. What this platform is

Amahirwe connects students with teachers, mentors, opportunity providers, parents/guardians and school administrators. Accounts, talent assessments, mentor matches and opportunities shown in this prototype may include illustrative demo data and are not guaranteed to reflect real people, organisations or outcomes.

2. Creating an account

  • You must provide accurate information when registering, including a real email address you control.
  • Accounts for teachers and opportunity providers are subject to admin verification before full access is granted.
  • You're responsible for keeping your password confidential and for activity on your account.
  • Students under 18 are expected to use the platform with awareness of their school or guardian, consistent with Amahirwe's role as a school-anchored program.

3. Acceptable use

You agree not to:

  • Impersonate another person or misrepresent your role (student, teacher, mentor, provider or parent).
  • Use the platform to harass, mislead or exploit another user, particularly across the student/mentor relationship.
  • Attempt to bypass account verification, access controls or the email-verification step.
  • Post opportunities, messages or content that are fraudulent, unsafe, or not genuinely intended for students.

4. Mentorship and opportunities

Mentor matches are intended to be reviewed by a teacher or administrator before a connection is considered active. Opportunities (scholarships, competitions, internships, training) are provided by third parties where noted; Amahirwe does not guarantee eligibility, availability or outcomes for any listed opportunity.

5. Prototype limitations

This build is a demonstration project. Features such as email delivery, payment processing, or production-grade content moderation may be simulated or incomplete. Data may be reset, and uptime is not guaranteed.

6. Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated as the prototype evolves. Continued use of the platform after a change means you accept the updated terms.

7. Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent through the contact page.
